   stdout. Source mechanism is not an observed failed restore. Require complete
   independent capture/readback; do not invent a transport repair or timing
   workaround before evidence. Address restore migration/adoption changes in
   the oracle, rather than falsely requiring unrelated fields to be identical.

   what it qualifies from later integrated browser/item/save verification and
