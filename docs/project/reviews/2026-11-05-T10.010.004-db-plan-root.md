## Personally checked corrections

1. **Image pruning:** app cmds.rs2120 calls prune_old_images at the end of up,
   even when ensure_images returned for an absent bundle. Admission must check
   the complete current image/tag census before up and its preservation after;
   absent bundle alone is insufficient.
2. **Era ownership:** unexpected pre-renewal volume presence refuses this run.
   with_era_database can stop/recreate a DB, rewrite its marker and migrate
   credentials. Do not silently visit another era or call that a read-only
   operation. The known owned baseline contains only the admitted Renewal data.
3. **Quiescence:** ordinary up is followed by exact-ID TERM/exited0 proofs for
   web, map, char and login, while keeping the admitted DB running. All oracle,
   backup, fixture-apply and restore steps happen afterward. No game service may
   restart during them. A later exited0 state cannot prove an earlier app
   stop did not escalate. This also avoids explaining away player-password
   changes caused by unnecessary login restarts.
4. **Complete oracle:** inventory every current table and non-table object.
   Compare complete table schemas, all rows and relevant sequence state;
   compare full routine/function/trigger/view/event definitions when present,
   or prove the relevant sets empty. Names/counts and selected cells do not
   establish whole-data preservation. Captured definitions cannot be discarded
   by the comparison. Normalizations must be named and justified individually.
5. **Completion:** use an explicit completion marker emitted only after each
   successful dump/query, cover it in the captured hash/size manifest and require
   explicit terminal exec success. A valid hash of a partial file is still a
   partial file. The ordinary dump comment cannot be SNAP's marker when its
   command disables comments. [MariaDB's option reference](https://mariadb.com/docs/server/clients-and-utilities/backup-restore-and-import-clients/mariadb-dump)
   documents that comments are disabled by skip-comments and the completion-date
   comment depends on comments being enabled. The app backup uses a different
   command; its own dump-footer check remains separate.
6. **WC claim:** the client loses raw multiplexed bytes and its decoder does
   not validate all frame/error/terminal conditions. It is not merely a decimal
   string-prefix operation. Under narrower immutable-source/exclusive-truncated-
   destination/no-duplicate-input assumptions, a shortened genuine count does
   not become the larger expected count; losing whitespace need not lose any
   digit. Remove the universal integrity guarantee. Require independent restored
   semantics and retain the actual guard observation separately.
7. **Two-stage admission:** host/offline stopped-state admission comes first;
   current Engine images/network/volumes/containers are checked only after
   controlled VM boot. Do not claim fresh live Engine inventory while its
   socket is stopped. Then admit ordinary up against those exact bindings.

## Corrected Root execution contract

One fresh Root-owned phase, after executable review/admission, will:

1. Rebind stopped VM/config/disks,1109-file world, source/runtime/image inputs,
   resources, explicit environment and unchanged five personal projections.
   Boot the owned VM, inspect its complete exact baseline, then ordinary up.
2. Prove current service identities/images and quiesce the four game services
   with full-ID TERM/exited0 evidence. Keep the same Renewal DB/network/volume.
3. Capture complete live schema/object/data snapshot S0. Inventory actual
   column/default/engine/sequence and stored-object interfaces before deciding
   the concrete fixture. Preserve a supported original backup B0.
4. Apply a small valid nonempty login/character/inventory/quest fixture through
   the supported DB-browser interface, with its safety backup preserved.
   Capture S1 and supported B1; verify exactly the admitted fixture differences.
5. Apply an explicit observable mutation, capture S2, restore B1 and capture S3.
   Require S1=S3 for all preserved data/schema and full fixture values, while
   checking that the safety dump contains the pre-restore mutation. Only the
   specifically justified local-service credential adoption can be excepted;
   player credentials/data are not a blanket exclusion.
6. Restore B0, capture S4 and require original S0 equivalence plus fixture
   absence. Preserve all baseline/fixture/safety dumps. No automatic recovery
   cascade on ambiguity or repeated transport attempts is authorized.
7. Reconcile all host writes/transients, protected files, image/network/volume/
   DB identities and personal projections. Prove final DB TERM/exited0 and clean
   log before supported down; prove the owned VM stopped. Unknown actor state
   or unsettled CLI means preserve and report, without forced VM/DB shutdown.

Completion of a normal restore does not expose the deleted staged file or
independently reveal the WC guard input. That observation gate remains open.
Root/Astra source-review a narrowly scoped CLI observer design. The supported
Docker-path override permits passing ordinary calls straight through and
observing only the exact restore WC call. Match full argv and invoking app PID,
bind the real CLI/environment/operation/source dump, retain separate raw streams
and termination evidence, then forward unchanged bytes only after the receipt
is safely retained. Evidence failure intentionally refuses before import;
it must be labeled an instrumentation refusal. No adapter is implemented or
admitted yet. It must not fix output, retry, substitute values or alter package
files. The extra process/pipes change scheduling, so this qualifies only the
instrumented supported restore, not an uninstrumented timing guarantee or intact
underlying multiplexed frames. Semantic comparison remains required.

Before use, Root requires isolated controls for exact/near-miss dispatch and
PID/path binding; pass-through argv/environment/stdin; byte forwarding for
numeric/whitespace/empty/non-UTF8/simultaneous streams and nonzero exit; child
signal/cancellation/overflow; receipt-write/duplicate failure; and a local guard
harness proving failed evidence retention prevents import. No child may be
abandoned, no signal may be relabeled a normal exit, and no truncated capture
may qualify. Until these controls and the complete executable are checked,
this plan authorizes **no runtime action**.

All proposed bounds are finite and must become exact constants in the source
admission, including copy/log thresholds and cancellation handling. Existing
4GiB/CPU2 limits, internet-on, Chinese inputs and personal-state preservation
remain mandatory. A future pass closes only the disposable DB rehearsal; it
does not prove browser/item/NPC/save/full-world behavior or personal recovery.
