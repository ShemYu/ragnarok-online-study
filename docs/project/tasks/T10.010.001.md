## Problem and selected interim policy

Native checkweight uses pc_checkadditem's first same-ID stack. Ordinary
getitem/pc_additem require matching bound, expiry, unique ID and cards before
merging. Thus a full bag containing only an incompatible same-ID coin stack
can pass preflight, replace C18370 with D18371 and request EXP before coin
delivery fails. A late hold is honest but does not prevent this predictable
case. Simply requiring a free slot or switching to checkweight2 is insufficient.

Implement this explicit conservative policy in the real Maysel NPC text:

1. After the last dialogue yield and exact full C-stage check, but before
   checkweight, changequest or either reward, refuse if either
   countitem("Ch1_Root_Coin") !=0 or rentalcountitem("Ch1_Root_Coin") !=0.
2. Give a distinct truthful short Traditional Chinese explanation that this
   interim path cannot safely handle coins already in inventory. Keep C and
   attempt no rewards. Do not describe an almost-empty bag as full, claim
   corruption, promise trading/storage/drop is possible, or move/delete items.
3. If both counts are zero, retain native checkweight("Ch1_Root_Coin",10),
   existing coin snapshot, checked C→D replacement, getexp then getitem, and
   all quantity/tuple holds. No new yield in the write/reward window.
4. Keep D repeats read-only, including after an unforeseen late delivery
   failure. No automatic reward replay, compensation, rollback to C or new
   permanent marker. No SQL/crash atomicity or universal delivery claim.

Both plain count builtins sum positive matching inventory rows regardless of
bound/cards/unique ID; ordinary count covers zero expiry, rental count covers
positive expiry, including expired-but-retained rows. Under valid positive
inventory quantities, nonnegative expiry, nonoverflowing counts, attached
actor and stable qualified DB/alias, both zero exclude a same-ID stack.
Retained checkweight then checks a new stack's weight/usable free slot. Native
execution/current inventory occurrence/installed DB identity remain unverified.
Malformed zero/negative quantity structures are outside this proof, not repair
targets. Do not remove checkweight: missing aliases must still refuse there.

This **also refuses compatible existing stacks**, with or without a spare
slot. Preserve their original native-positive outcomes in the record and
label the new refusal as policy narrowing. Do not silently call it equivalent
capacity behavior, full stack support or full parent acceptance. Documentation
must state this restriction and link the future exact-capacity requirement.
