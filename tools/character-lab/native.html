What RO’s modular template actually contains SPR drawings + ACT animation data

The drawings and the instructions.

SPR stores the sprite artwork. ACT selects drawings over time and records their positions, transforms, timing, and attachment anchors. The body and clothes are one module; head, headgear, weapon, and effects are separate modules.

Body poses are complete drawings. An ACT does not pose a single unposed character. A replacement costume needs artwork for the sprite slots used by the action, with compatible alignment.

This small test.

Local male Swordsman action 86: the east / southeast dagger attack, reconstructed from the available ACT and SPR data. Its nine frames use five unique body drawings and one head drawing, held for 96 ms each. The source is retained, including its repeated drawings and final follow-through.

The Wayfarer head and clothes follow those pose templates. Separate equipment follows its source action data. The original head remains selectable; hats, weapon and effects are reference assets. This is a local reconstruction, not footage captured from the official client.
